Privacy policy

HomePrint by High Point Perspective · Effective November 19, 2026

HomePrint helps homeowners find where photos of their home are still public online, and how to get them removed. This page explains what we collect, why, and who we share it with. We never sell your data, and we never show ads.

What we collect

  • The address you scan, and the public web pages we find for it.
  • Your email address, if you create an account. You can sign in with an email link or with Google. From Google we receive only your name, email and profile picture, and we use only your email.
  • Your removal progress: which pages you've marked as requested or removed, notes you add, and the name you choose to put in removal letters.
  • Payment records: which plan you bought and when. Card details go straight to Stripe. We never see or store them.
  • Basic security data: a one-way hash of your IP address, used to limit abuse and kept only briefly. We don't store your raw IP address.
  • Reviews you choose to leave. Reviews are always optional.

How we use it

  • To run scans, show your report, and re-check your home on the schedule your plan includes.
  • To sign you in and keep your saved homes in your account.
  • To send emails about your account and your report. That includes the sign-in link and at most one optional request for a review.
  • To prevent abuse, such as automated scanning.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers, only to run HomePrint:

  • Bolt / Supabase: hosting, database and sign-in.
  • Serper: sends the address you scan as a web search query to find public pages.
  • Stripe: payments.
  • Google: only if you choose "Continue with Google".
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: bot protection on scans.
  • Resend: sending email.

We don't send removal requests on your behalf. You send them yourself, so nothing goes to a listing site, agent or MLS unless you choose to send it.

Cookies and storage

We use your browser's storage to keep you signed in, remember your language, and connect a scan you ran before signing in to your account. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your account data while your account is open. Email hello@homeprint.online from the address on your account, and we'll delete your account, saved homes and reports within 30 days. We may keep payment records where the law requires it.

Children

HomePrint is for adults and isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll tell signed-in users by email.
